04Panic SalonPluck gray hairs, trim bangs, and restore hair. Find out if you’re a star stylist—or a salon disaster.

  • Three tasks, three skillsGrab the right hair, cut an even fringe, and inject just the right dose of growth serum.
  • Rookie mistakes, big laughsOne wrong pluck or crooked cut turns a simple service into a hilarious mess.
  • Customers with characterDramatic reactions make every makeover—and mishap—worth watching.
  • Take turns, share the chaosPass the phone between clients. Run the salon together or compete to be the best stylist.

An experiment in 3D gameplay: Built to test whether ASM could power a complete, self-looping game. Panic Salon demonstrates three distinct interactions within one playable loop.
